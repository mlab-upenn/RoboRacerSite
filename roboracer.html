About the Course

Not just another robotics course. This is autonomous driving under race conditions.

RoboRacer (formerly F1TENTH) is a graduate-level project course at Penn Engineering where teams build and program 1/10th-scale autonomous race cars. Instead of exams, your grade is earned on the track.

You'll implement a complete autonomy stack — reactive control, SLAM-based mapping, path planning, and vision-based perception — all deployed on real hardware at real speed.

Taught by Prof. Rahul Mangharam, adopted by over 90 universities across 6 continents.

  • ⚡

    Hands-On From Day One

    Simulation and real hardware run in parallel. No waiting until the end to touch a robot.

  • 🏁

    Competition-Based Learning

    Three races replace exams. Same hardware for everyone — only algorithms decide the winner.

  • 🔬

    Research-Grade Topics

    SLAM, particle filters, MPC, RRT, neural perception — directly applicable to industry.

  • 🌐

    Open Source Platform

    All materials, code, and hardware designs are open. Join a global ROBORACER community.

Instructor & Staff

Rahul Mangharam
Professor · ESE & CIS · University of Pennsylvania

Prof. Mangharam is a founding member of the PRECISE Center and director of the Safe Autonomous Systems Lab at Penn. He created RoboRacer (formerly F1TENTH) in 2016 to make autonomous vehicle education hands-on, competitive, and accessible. What started as a small event has grown into a global platform adopted by over 90 universities.

His research focuses on safe autonomous systems — combining machine learning performance with formal safety guarantees for real-world deployment in vehicles, medical devices, and energy systems.
